THE UNIVERSITY — EXAMINATION REGULATIONS
Issued by the examinations office, Building 1
Approved by the academic board, revised April 2026

1. ELIGIBILITY
1.1 A student may sit the examination for a course only if attendance in that
    course is at least two thirds of the scheduled classes.
1.2 Fees for the current semester must be settled before the first paper.

2. AT THE EXAMINATION
2.1 Bring your student card. A student sitting without one is admitted
    provisionally and must present it to the examinations office within three
    working days.
2.2 Arrive fifteen minutes before the start time. A student arriving more than
    twenty minutes after the start is not admitted; a student may not leave in
    the first thirty minutes.
2.3 Only the materials named on the question paper may be brought to the desk.
    Where a dictionary is permitted, it must be a printed one.
2.4 Phones and watches are placed in the tray at the front of the room, switched
    off, for the duration of the paper.

3. ABSENCE
3.1 Absence through illness requires a medical certificate submitted within one
    week. A resit is then scheduled in the following examination period.
3.2 Absence for any other reason is at the discretion of the faculty office and
    must be applied for in advance where the circumstances allow.

4. MISCONDUCT
4.1 Suspected misconduct is reported to the academic board, which may annul the
    paper, annul every paper in the semester, or suspend the student.
4.2 The student is heard by the board before a decision is made.

5. RESULTS AND APPEALS
5.1 Results are published on the student portal on the dates in the academic
    calendar.
5.2 An appeal against a mark is made in writing to the faculty office within two
    weeks of publication, and must state the grounds. A mark is not revised on
    the ground of disagreement with academic judgement alone.
