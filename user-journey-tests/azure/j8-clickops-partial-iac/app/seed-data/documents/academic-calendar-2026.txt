THE UNIVERSITY — ACADEMIC CALENDAR 2026
Issued by the registrar's office, Building 1

FIRST SEMESTER
  1 April          Academic year begins
  6 April          Entrance ceremony; course registration opens
  17 April         Course registration closes (17:00)
  20 April         Ballot results posted for capped seminar courses
  29 April         Public holiday — no classes
  1 June           Foundation Day — no classes
  20-31 July       First-semester examinations
  1 August         First semester ends

SUMMER RECESS
  1 August         Recess begins
  10-14 August     Offices closed
  20 September     Recess ends

SECOND SEMESTER
  21 September     Second semester begins; registration opens
  2 October        Course registration closes (17:00)
  6-8 November     University festival — no classes
  25 December      Second-semester classes end
  26 Dec - 6 Jan   Winter recess; offices closed
  7 January        Classes resume
  26 Jan - 6 Feb   Second-semester examinations
  20 February      Results published
  20 March         Graduation ceremony
  31 March         Academic year ends

Dates are confirmed unless marked provisional. Changes are posted on the
student portal notice board and take precedence over this document.
