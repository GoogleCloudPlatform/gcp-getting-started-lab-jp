# Global Bond Ladder Fund

Investment-grade government and corporate bonds arranged as a ladder with rungs at one, three, five, seven and ten years, fully hedged to yen. The ladder is the reason the fund exists: it gives a predictable maturity schedule rather than a single duration number that moves with the market.

The fund is the ballast in the Conservative and Balanced profiles and shrinks toward zero in the most aggressive one. Because the hedge is complete, the fund's yen return is close to the underlying yield minus the hedging cost, and that cost has been the largest drag on the fund in every year since launch.

Ongoing charge is 0.19 per cent a year. Distributions are accumulated.
