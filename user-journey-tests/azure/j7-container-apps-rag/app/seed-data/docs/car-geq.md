# Global Equity Index Fund

A market-capitalisation weighted fund tracking developed-market large and mid cap equities, hedged back to yen on roughly half the exposure. It is the growth engine in every model portfolio and carries the largest weight in the Balanced and Growth profiles.

Holdings are rebalanced quarterly against the index rather than continuously, so a portfolio can drift by up to two percentage points between rebalances. The fund does not screen holdings on any sustainability criterion; customers who ask for that are steered to the Sustainable Leaders fund instead.

Ongoing charge is 0.16 per cent a year. The fund has no performance fee, no entry charge and no exit charge. Distributions are accumulated rather than paid out.
