# Yen Liquidity Reserve

Short-dated Japanese government bills and bank deposits, held at a constant weight of three per cent in every portfolio and topped up from whichever sleeve is furthest above its target at the quarterly rebalance.

The reserve exists so that a withdrawal request does not force a sale in a falling market. It is not a return-seeking holding and its yield is disclosed as approximately zero after charges. Customers who ask why they hold cash in a long-term portfolio are given exactly that answer.

Ongoing charge is 0.08 per cent a year. Distributions are accumulated.
