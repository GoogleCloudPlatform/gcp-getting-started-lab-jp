# Japan Equity Core Fund

Domestic Japanese equities across the TOPIX universe, with a modest tilt away from the very largest constituents so that a single trading house or car maker cannot dominate the sleeve. Every model portfolio holds some, on the argument that a customer who earns and spends in yen should not be entirely exposed to foreign currencies.

The weight rises with the customer's stated home bias, from eight per cent at the lowest setting to twenty-eight per cent at the highest. This is the single largest source of variation between two customers on the same risk profile.

Ongoing charge is 0.22 per cent a year. Distributions are accumulated.
