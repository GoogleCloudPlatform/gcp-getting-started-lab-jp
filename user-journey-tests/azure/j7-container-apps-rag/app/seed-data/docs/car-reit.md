# J-REIT Income Fund

Listed Japanese real estate investment trusts, weighted toward logistics and residential and away from retail and hotels. Held for income and for the diversification it gives against the equity sleeve, not as a substitute for owning property.

This is the most volatile holding in any portfolio despite sitting in the income bucket, and customers are shown that explicitly before it is added. The fund is capped at twelve per cent of any portfolio regardless of risk profile.

Ongoing charge is 0.34 per cent a year, the highest of the range, reflecting the smaller universe and the cost of trading it. Distributions are paid quarterly.
